About

I sit between the artists and the developers — preparing assets for code-based implementation, assembling and polishing scenes, and making all of it fit a budget that does not move.

I don't hand assets off and walk away. I build the tooling that makes them: an Electron tool wrapped around our playable template, procedural Blender systems, custom shaders. The repetitive half of creative production shouldn't be done by hand — twelve to thirteen playables a month is only survivable that way.

I also read the numbers. At Nexters Global I worked with CPI, CTR and retention and fed them back into art decisions. Creatives are testable hypotheses, not deliverables.
