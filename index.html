About

I'm a technical artist with 5+ years in mobile games, working where art meets engineering. Day to day I sit between the artists and the developers: preparing assets for code-based implementation, assembling and polishing scenes, and making the result fit a build budget that does not move.

I don't hand off assets and walk away. I build the tooling that produces them — an Electron tool around our playable template, procedural Blender systems, custom shaders — because the repetitive half of creative production should not be done by hand.

I also read the numbers. At Nexters Global I worked with CPI, CTR and retention and fed them back into art decisions. Creatives are testable hypotheses, not deliverables.
