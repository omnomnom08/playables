About

I do

click to spin

I'm a technical artist. I sit between the artists and the developers: preparing assets for code-based implementation, assembling and polishing scenes, and making all of it fit a budget that does not move.

I don't hand assets off and walk away. I build the tooling that makes them — an Electron tool wrapped around our playable template, procedural Blender systems, custom shaders — because the repetitive half of creative production shouldn't be done by hand. Twelve to thirteen playables a month is only survivable that way.

I also read the numbers. At Nexters Global I worked with CPI, CTR and retention and fed them back into art decisions. Creatives are testable hypotheses, not deliverables.

My degree is in optics engineering, which turns out to be a reasonable way into rendering.
